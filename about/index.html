Time just rugged itself.

Chaos watched his 25-word seed phrase tear loose and scatter across every era. You ride the rift with him, ducking behind painted cover, popping grunt Types, and peeling one word off a 1/1 boss at the end of each stage.

The words are not flavor text. Lined up, they spell a warning:

NOT YOUR KEYS. NOT YOUR COINS. NEVER TYPE THE PHRASE ON A WEBSITE. VERIFY TWICE. GAS FEES STING. RUGS PULL HARDER. HOLD THROUGH FUD. CHAOS.

Then the twist lands. The last face in the rift is a grinning lime-and-magenta copy of Chaos himself, the Chaos Echo, and the self-phish is the point. "Cute," he says. "You phished me with my own face."

Vera Vale, live from Time News: "If a glowing copy of you wants your seed, you are the rug."

The comic chase

A short comic-strip story, face-safe balloons, cinematic intro, stage cards, and Vera's sign-off. You recover the words. You never type them.

25 eras. 25 bosses.

Twenty-five short stages and twenty-five bosses painted from the official MetaWinners 1/1 likenesses, including the Chaos Echo. Mix of cover fights and rail chases: train, barge, yacht, neon highway, maglev, rover.

In the rift

Types, not paper dolls

Painted, animated MetaWinner Types in era dress: woods, west, Rome, Egypt, arcade, garage, and the rest of the rift. Visored citizens, not generic thugs.

Cover you hide behind

Mossy logs, arcade cabinets, wagon barrels, marble drums, sarcophagi, energy barriers, ice blocks. Different sizes and shapes per stage.

Click, hide, combo

Click or tap to shoot. Hold cover to reload. Don't shoot civilians. Chain headshots into a combo. Readable S/A/B/C grade, local high scores.

Built for the browser
